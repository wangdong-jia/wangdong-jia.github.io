Volatility on an Event Clock

Physical time moves uniformly. Markets do not.

What changes when price movement sets the clock? This EUR/USD case study transforms irregular threshold crossings into event-time price history, then uses causal filters to examine return volatility and rapid variation around scheduled economic releases.

Background

I am a PhD candidate in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at UC Berkeley. My work sits at the intersection of stochastic systems, optimization, learning, and game-theoretic models, with an emphasis on turning mathematical structure into computational methods. I study heterogeneous interacting systems and equilibrium computation through mathematical analysis and deep-learning-based numerical methods.

My earlier training in applied mathematics and physics provides a foundation for this work, connecting questions about uncertainty and interaction with the analysis and computation of mathematical models.
